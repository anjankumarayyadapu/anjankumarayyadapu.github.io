About

I bring 18+ years of overall IT experience, with 13+ years dedicated to Big Data, Hadoop, and hybrid cloud platform architecture as a Senior Solutions Architect at Cloudera.

My career spans Big Data engineering, DevOps, UNIX/Linux administration, and project management. For more than 13 years, I have specialized in Hadoop ecosystem platforms, multi-node cluster administration, and enterprise data architecture — from CDH and HDP through modern Cloudera CDP, Spark, Iceberg, and Edge-to-AI solutions.

I design hybrid data platforms, migrate legacy workloads to AWS and GCP, and deliver generative AI solutions with secure data governance. My work spans architecture, hands-on implementation, and executive-level customer consulting for regulated industries.

I hold certifications from AWS, IBM, Databricks, Google Cloud, Stanford, and PMI — including AWS Solutions Architect Professional and AWS Machine Learning Specialty. I am also a Fellow of the British Computer Society (FBCS), Docker Captain, Senior IEEE Member, peer reviewer, keynote speaker, Globee Awards judge, and published AI researcher with multiple patents.
